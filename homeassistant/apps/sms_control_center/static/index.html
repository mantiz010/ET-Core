What do the statuses mean?

Outgoing queue: observed on MQTT.
Service accepted: Home Assistant accepted the request.
Received: reported by the SMS bridge.
Unconfirmed: the result is uncertain; no automatic retry.
Request rejected: Home Assistant refused the request.

Outgoing messages are never marked delivered without a handset delivery report. Your current bridge does not supply those reports to this app.
